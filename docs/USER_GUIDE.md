# User guide

## Schedule a remittance

1. Open the calendar and choose a due date.
2. Select a saved recipient and review the destination address.
3. Enter the amount and create the payment reminder.
4. When due, open the payment action and connect Freighter.
5. Confirm the network is Mainnet, review the recipient and amount, then sign.

## Confirm settlement

Wait for a successful explorer result. The application must show the transaction
hash and the matching payment record before the reminder is considered complete.
Do not retry a pending payment with a new memo until the first hash is checked.
